Use it in video calls

Zoom, Teams, Google Meet and most call apps can't use a browser tab as a camera. The free OBS Studio app connects the two with its Virtual Camera.

  1. Install OBS Studio and open it.
  2. Here, press Pop out at the top of the preview and keep that small window visible. If your browser has no Pop out, use Focus view instead.
  3. In OBS, add a Window Capture source and pick that window.
  4. Press Start Virtual Camera in OBS.
  5. In your call app, choose OBS Virtual Camera as the camera.

To key yourself over slides or a game, pick Green screen and add a Chroma Key filter in OBS.
